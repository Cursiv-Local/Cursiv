CURSIV FOR MAC: HOW TO OPEN IT

For Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 12 or later.

1. Drag Cursiv into the Applications folder (in this window).

2. The first time only: this build isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS will say
   "Cursiv can't be opened" or "Apple could not verify Cursiv". To open it:
     - Try to open Cursiv once, and click Done (or OK).
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
       "Open Anyway" next to Cursiv. Confirm with your password.
   After that it opens normally.

   (Or, in Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Cursiv.app )

3. In Cursiv, the Setup window offers to install Ollama, the free engine that runs
   the AI model on your Mac, and to download the model (about 2.5 GB). That needs
   internet once. After that everything works offline.

How to check this download: the release page lists its SHA-256 checksum
(SHA256SUMS-mac.txt). In Terminal:
    shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/Cursiv-*-mac-arm64.dmg

Status: the Mac version is built and tested automatically on Apple's hardware at
GitHub, but hasn't yet been tried by a person on their own Mac. The Cursiv USB drive
is Windows-only. Please report anything that doesn't work:
https://github.com/winklersllc2026-bit/Cursiv/issues
