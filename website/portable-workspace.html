Your notebook, in your pocket

A portable research notebook that runs from a USB drive

Cursiv can make a drive that carries the program, the local AI engine, a model and your field notebook. Plug it into a Windows computer, start Cursiv from the drive, work, and take everything with you. Nothing needs to be installed on the computer you borrow.

How it works

  1. Make a driveFrom Cursiv's menu, "Make a Cursiv USB…" (Windows): the program, the local AI engine and, if you want it, a model. Your private records aren't copied unless you choose to include them.
  2. Start from the driveCursiv keeps its data on the drive, so the computer keeps nothing of yours.
  3. Move between computersThe drive letter can change; paths on the drive are stored relative to it.
  4. Bring data backNotebook entries from other laptops or phones are imported with their signatures and chain checked; conflicting histories are kept side by side, never merged.

Encryption, plainly

On a computer, conversations, memories, the field notebook, photos and phone uploads are encrypted on disk. A Cursiv USB drive stays readable, by design, so it works on any computer: anyone who has the drive can read it. Use full-drive encryption (such as BitLocker To Go) if you carry sensitive records.

Every save writes a temporary file and swaps it in, so an unplugged drive leaves the old version or the new one, never half of a file. Recovery procedures.

What's tested

An end-to-end USB test (make the drive, start from it, move it, bring the data back) runs on every change and must pass before any release. Each Windows release is started from a USB layout before it's published. The developer has moved data computer to drive to another computer, and from an external SSD, on real hardware. Mac drives aren't supported for this yet. Supported platforms.
