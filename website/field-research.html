Field research

Record it. Keep the original.

Observations, measurements and photos in a notebook that preserves correction history.

Built around the record

  • Keep sites, sample IDs, units, coordinates and notes together.
  • Add a linked correction instead of overwriting an observation.
  • Check record history and export signed copies.
  • Use supported field calculations without asking a model to do the arithmetic.

See the notebook

Work alone or bring a team

Use a prepared Windows drive offline, then bring named notebooks back to a main computer. See the team-drive demo.

U106 also offers confirmation previews for supported plain-language observations. AI helpers can assist with writing and questions; verify their answers against the records.

A tamper-evident chain checks record consistency, not whether an observation is true. Read the integrity and retrieval reference for the full limits.
