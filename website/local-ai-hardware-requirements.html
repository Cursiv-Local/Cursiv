Will it run on your computer?

Start with 8 GB RAM and room for the app, Ollama and a model. 16 GB RAM gives you more room.

A practical starting point

  • Windows: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • Mac: Apple Silicon, macOS 12+; early build.
  • Storage: about 4–8 GB free for the runtime and one small model; more for your files.
  • Graphics: optional. Compatible GPU acceleration can improve speed.

Choose the model, then test

Setup recommends qwen3:4b-instruct, about a 2.5 GB download. A smaller model can suit limited memory. Larger models and longer conversations need more resources.

CPU-only use is possible, but can be slower. No single response time applies to every computer. Try your own documents and questions before relying on a setup.
