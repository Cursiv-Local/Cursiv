The story so far

Built by doing the work.

Cursiv grew from a general AI workspace into a local tool for documents, observations and detailed records.

A clearer focus

Public development dates back to May 2026. Today the focus is free, open-source local AI, tamper-evident notebooks and portable research workspaces.

The aim is practical: help researchers, technicians and independent workers keep useful records without depending on a connection.

Follow the current project

Joshua Winkler develops Cursiv in public. Release evidence, code and known limitations are available in the current repository.
