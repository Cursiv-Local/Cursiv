Open-source · local-first AI for Windows

Local intelligence. Even without the internet.

Cursiv runs an AI model on your own computer, keeps its memory and reference material there, and checks its answers before giving them. Online AI services are optional extras you turn on, not a requirement.

Connectivity is optional. Trust is not.

Cursiv in five minutes

Six things it does, shown on real answers.

Each screen below is an unedited answer from the current release, running offline on a laptop with a 4 GB graphics card. Nothing was typed in afterwards.

  1. 1

    Works offline, and shows its source

    The green pill says Offline mode: Cursiv makes no internet connections. For a survival or first-aid question, the reference entry it's using is shown word for word above the answer, with where it comes from (here, the U.S. EPA) and when that was last checked.

  2. 2

    Emergencies come first

    A medical emergency gets "call 911" before anything else, then the few steps that matter, from the reference card. If an answer ever contradicts the card, Cursiv flags it under the answer.

  3. 3

    Says "I don't know" instead of inventing

    Asked about a study that doesn't exist, or something it can't know, it says so plainly. It doesn't make up a summary or claim to have searched a database.

  4. 4

    Exact numbers and exact formats

    Clock times and sums are worked out by Cursiv itself, not guessed by the model. Ask for exactly four steps, one sentence each, and that's what you get.

  5. 5

    Notes that come back

    Say "remember …" and Cursiv keeps it on the computer, marked as something you said, with the date. It's still there after a restart; an automated test checks that on every change.

  6. 6

    Asks when a question is unclear

    "Is it safe?" with nothing to say what "it" is gets a short question back, not a guess. (Shown in dark mode; Cursiv can follow Windows' light or dark setting.)

Recorded with scripts/site_shots.py: the questions run through Cursiv on a fresh, offline copy and the replies are saved unedited in answers.json, with the release and date.

Testing and results

Measured, not assumed.

The Cursiv Truth Benchmark asks the same fixed questions on every release and scores the answers with code, not with another AI.

  • 125 cases in 9 categories: general knowledge, reasoning, numbers, reference use, memory, unclear questions, false premises and traps, safety, and questions Cursiv can't know.
  • Scored by fixed rules: the right answer or number, invented details, admitting "I can't know", asking when the question is unclear, using the right reference entry.
  • Run clean: a fresh copy, no keys, no memories, offline mode on.
  • Frozen: the questions and checks are versioned (now suite 1.2). A check is only changed when it was wrong, the change is written down, and earlier releases are scored again on the new version.

When information can leave your computer

Online only when you turn something on.

With default settings and no keys, everyday chat stays on your computer. These are the only things that send data, and each is off or manual until you use it.

Sends data only when you use it

  • AI keys you add: your message goes to that provider when the local model can't answer, or for images.
  • Cursiv Cloud: off by default; if turned on, used only when no local model is ready.
  • Web search: only for messages starting with search:, or if you turn on automatic search.
  • Update check: when you choose Check for Updates (tray icon), or if you turn on automatic checks.
  • Phone app: its conversation and photos are kept in your space on this website.
  • Problem reports: sent only when you click Send.

Offline mode

  • Blocks all of the above and any other connection off the computer, at the network-socket level.
  • Stays on after a restart until you turn it off.
  • Keeps the local model, memory, reference, letters and saved conversations working.

Evidence: automated tests and a live chat-turn audit with zero outside connections. A full feature-by-feature offline demonstration and packet-level capture are still to be published.

Known limitations

What Cursiv can't do yet.

Small local models make mistakes

A 4B model running on a home PC can be confidently wrong. The answer check catches some errors, not all. Check anything that matters.

Not a medical or safety system

About a third of the reference is written from public U.S. government guidance; the rest has no published source yet. None of it has been reviewed by a medical professional. In an emergency, call emergency services.

Not certified

Cursiv is not certified for medical, safety-critical, aerospace or spaceflight use. Those would need independent evaluation and validation.

Disk encryption

The login keeps people at the keyboard out; it doesn't encrypt memories or conversations on disk. Saved AI keys are encrypted.

Windows only, unsigned

No Mac or Linux desktop version yet. The installer isn't code-signed, so Windows warns about an unknown publisher.

Speed depends on hardware

On a laptop with a 4 GB graphics card, a typical everyday answer takes 5–30 seconds (benchmark average: 25 s on U63, before U64 roughly doubled the speed); Deep Think takes a minute or two. Without a graphics card it's slower.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Cursiv free?

Yes. Cursiv is open source under the MIT license. Free Gemini and Groq keys are optional; paid keys only if you want them.

Do I need to be online?

Once, to download Ollama and a model during setup. After that, with a local model installed, everyday use works without internet.

Windows warned me about the installer. Is it safe?

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows shows "unknown publisher". Click More info → Run anyway. The source code it's built from is public.

Is the AI always right?

No. It can be confidently wrong, especially with a small local model. Cursiv checks numbers and sources and says when it can't know, but check anything important, especially health, money and legal questions.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. The desktop app is Windows-only today. The phone app works on any phone.
