Direction

Trustworthy first. Then bigger.

Cursiv is meant to become a local intelligence system that keeps reliable knowledge even when connectivity, hardware, information or the AI itself are unreliable. It gets there one level at a time, and each level has to be shown to work before the next one is built.

Connectivity is optional. Trust is not.

What "trustworthy" means here

Honest about what it knows.

Not "always right". No AI is. The goal is that Cursiv is always clear about what it knows, what it inferred, what it's assuming, and what it can't tell, and that it says "I don't have enough evidence" instead of inventing an answer.

Kinds of knowledge kept apart

  • Memory: what a person told Cursiv
  • Reference: curated material, with its source
  • Observation: something a person or instrument recorded
  • Inference: a conclusion drawn from the above
  • Hypothesis: a possible explanation, not established
  • Unknown: not enough evidence

Today Cursiv separates memory origin and Known / Inferred / Assumed / Unknown in answers. Observations and hypotheses as records are planned.

Not the goal

What Cursiv isn't trying to be.

  • Another general chatbot competing on personality or size.
  • Features added because they sound futuristic.
  • A system that claims perfect accuracy, or certification it doesn't have. Cursiv is not certified for medical, safety-critical, aerospace or spaceflight use.
  • A product that needs the cloud. Online services stay optional.
  • A distributed system built before the single-computer version is shown to be reliable.
