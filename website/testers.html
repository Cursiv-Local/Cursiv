Help make Cursiv easier to use.

Try a real task and tell me what felt wrong, slow or confusing. No developer experience needed.

A useful report includes

  • What you tried and the exact steps or prompt.
  • What you expected and what happened.
  • Your Cursiv version, operating system and online/offline state.
  • A screenshot or reply, with private details removed.

Send it directly

cursiv.local@yahoo.com · Open a GitHub issue

Feedback informs development. There is no formal program, payment or guarantee a suggestion will ship.
