Help test Cursiv

Your notes go straight into how the next version is built.

Download Cursiv for Windows Setup help and Mac download

Cursiv is a local-first assistant focused on field notes, offline use and checkable answers.

Testing means trying real tasks—logging an observation, asking a question offline or checking a calculation—and telling me what felt off, slow or unclear.

You do not need to be a developer. Clear descriptions of what you did and what happened are enough.

I read feedback and use it to decide what gets fixed next. There is no formal program, no payment and no guarantee every suggestion ships. Direct tester notes have already changed parsing, restrictions and source labeling.

What helps most

  • Exact prompts or steps you tried
  • What you expected vs what happened
  • Screenshots or copy-pasted replies when something looked wrong
  • Your OS and whether you were online or offline
  • Anything that felt confusing even if it technically worked

How to send it

Email cursiv.local@yahoo.com, or open a GitHub issue on Cursiv-Local/Cursiv.

Including your Cursiv version is useful. Find it in the app’s about line or ask “what is your version?”

Thanks for looking. Even one clear report is useful.
